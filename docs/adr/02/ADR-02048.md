# ADR-02048: The conformance runner admits each clang against the budget

- **Date**: 2026-11-22

## Context

- A compile reserved 256 MB at codegen and grew, without waiting, to 12×
  its IR once the IR existed (ADR-01060's budget). A library-heavy WPT or
  Node file's clang peaks at 3–4 GiB, past that factor; fourteen of them
  started side by side on Node304 (~60 GB), since none waited.

## Decision

- Right before its clang runs, a compile is admitted at its reservation
  (`admitClang`): it waits while it does not fit beside the others, unless
  no clang is running, so the waits cannot form a cycle.
- Each finished clang's peak resident size (rusage, Unix) per IR byte
  raises the run's factor (`irFactor`, at least 12, at most 64), so the
  later compiles of such programs are reserved at what the earlier ones
  took. Windows keeps its job-object cap.

## Consequences

- Tests: `TestMemBudgetAdmitClang`, `TestIRFactorFromPeak`.
