# ADR-01911: a growable buffer in the oracle

- **Relations**: `Implements TDD-00230` (P3.3 shadow buckets)
- **Date**: 2026-10-21

## Context

- Codegen keeps a buffer constructed with `{ maxByteLength }` growable on its type, a fact about the value; the oracle answered the plain buffer at the construction, its binding and each read.

## Decision

- The oracle marks the construction growable, and an unannotated binding of it at its declaration and reads.

## Consequences

- The typed-array example's rows go away.
