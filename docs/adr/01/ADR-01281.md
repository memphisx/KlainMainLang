# ADR-01281: `buffer`'s module-only exports

- **Relations**: `Implements part of TDD-00230` (P3.1), `Extends ADR-00666`
- **Date**: 2026-09-29

## Context

`import … from 'buffer'` reached only the globals it re-exports: `Buffer`,
`Blob`, `atob` and `btoa`. What Node's `lib/buffer.js` adds on top was
rejected as "no exported member":

- `constants` and `kMaxLength`/`kStringMaxLength`;
- `INSPECT_MAX_BYTES`;
- `isUtf8`, `isAscii` and `transcode`;
- `SlowBuffer`.

Namespace and default imports were rejected too ("not yet supported").

## Decision

- `lib/node/internal_buffer.ts`, the module's companion, holds those
  exports:
  - the constants have Node v24's values;
  - `isUtf8` validates as simdutf does, rejecting overlong forms,
    surrogates and code points past U+10FFFF;
  - both `isUtf8` and `isAscii` take an ArrayBuffer, a Buffer or another
    view, and throw Node's `ERR_INVALID_ARG_TYPE` for anything else;
  - `transcode` decodes and re-encodes;
  - `SlowBuffer` is `Buffer.allocUnsafeSlow`.
- A re-export module with a TypeScript companion takes a namespace or a
  default import.
- Through the namespace, the companion's exports are its declarations, and
  the primary exports are the globals (`buffer.Buffer` is `Buffer`).
- Still open (BACKLOG):
  - `File` does not exist as a global yet;
  - `resolveObjectURL` needs `URL.createObjectURL`'s registry.

## Verification

Mac (Apple Silicon): `TestE2EBufferModuleExtras` and
`examples/buffer/module.ts` print what Node v24.19.0 prints.
