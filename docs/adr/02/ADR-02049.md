# ADR-02049: A thread's TLS roots are added outside dl_iterate_phdr

- **Date**: 2026-11-22

## Context

- Under `-mm=gc` on Linux, about 1 in 300 runs of
  `TestE2EGCModeGoroutinesSelectChurn` (`GC_FREE_SPACE_DIVISOR=1000`)
  hung: the collecting thread waited in `dl_iterate_phdr` on the loader
  lock.
- A thread that runs emitted code registers its TLS blocks as roots
  (`__kml_gc_tls_register`). It called `GC_add_roots` from inside its
  `dl_iterate_phdr` callback: it held the loader lock while taking Boehm's
  allocation lock. A collection holds the allocation lock and calls
  `dl_iterate_phdr` to register the libraries' data: the two waited on each
  other whenever a goroutine's thread started during a collection.

## Decision

- The callback only gathers the calling thread's TLS blocks; their roots
  are added (removed, at thread exit) after `dl_iterate_phdr` returns, so no
  allocator lock is taken under the loader lock.

## Consequences

- The select-churn program ran 1600 times on Node304 (Linux x86-64, four
  lanes of 400, `GC_FREE_SPACE_DIVISOR=1000`) without a hang; before the
  change it hung within about 400.
