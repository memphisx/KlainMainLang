# ADR-01084: Date fields by integer arithmetic; expanded-year formatting

- **Date**: 2026-09-23

## Context

`examples/date/date.ts` crashed on Windows (`0xC0000005`) at `(start - oneDayMs).toISOString()`. Any read of a date before 1970 crashed there: `new Date(-1).toISOString()`, `getUTCFullYear()`, and the rest.

## Investigation

- `__kml_date_decompose` (`codegen/llvm/runtime_date.go`) called `gmtime` and read `struct tm` through the returned pointer. The Windows CRT's `gmtime` returns NULL for a negative `time_t`, so the read went through NULL.
- `gmtime` also returns a static buffer, which is not safe for Worker threads.
- The inverse direction already avoided libc: `__kml_days_from_civil` (Hinnant).
- Compared against Node 24 (`TZ=UTC`) on 16 edge timestamps, two formatting divergences remained once the crash was gone:
  - `toISOString` must print a year outside 0..9999 in the expanded form, a sign plus six digits: `-000001-01-01…`, `+275760-09-13…`;
  - `toDateString` must print a negative year as `-0001`. `%04lld` gives `-001`.

## Decision

- `__kml_date_decompose` computes the fields in integer IR:
  - floor-divide the milliseconds into days plus the second of the day;
  - derive hour, minute and second from the second of the day;
  - weekday = `(days + 4) mod 7`;
  - Hinnant's `civil_from_days` for year, month and day.
  
  No `gmtime`, so it is valid for the whole ±8.64e15 ms range, on every platform, and on every thread.
- `emitDateYearSign` splits the year into its printed sign and absolute value:
  - `toISOString` formats `%s%0*lld` with width 6 when the year is outside 0..9999;
  - `toDateString` formats `%s%04lld`.

## Implementation notes

`codegen/llvm/runtime_date.go`, `codegen/llvm/emit_date.go`. The `{ i64 x 8 }` result shape is unchanged, so every accessor keeps working.

## Side effects discovered

`toDateString`/`toLocaleDateString` are UTC here and local time in Node. This is the existing local-time `Date` gap, already tracked. The Node-comparison test therefore leaves `toDateString` out.

## Verification

- Windows x64: 16 edge timestamps are identical to Node 24 under `TZ=UTC`. They cover before 1970, year 1, year −1, leap day 2000, 1900, and both ends of the range. `examples/date/date.ts` exits 0.
- `tests/date_test.go`:
  - `TestE2EDatePreEpochAndExpandedYears`, a fixed expectation including the `toDateString` forms;
  - `TestE2EDatePreEpochAndExpandedYearsSameAsNode`.
- Linux x86-64 (Docker): the full suite and the examples were re-run.
