# ADR-01352: DataView is a TypeScript class

- **Relations**: Implements part of TDD-00243 and TDD-00230 P3.4; builds on ADR-01351
- **Date**: 2026-10-03

## Context

DataView was a host type: the `NewDataViewExpression` node, the `IsDataView`
flag and a 4-word heap struct, with dedicated codegen for 22 accessors, three
properties, inspect and `instanceof`. TDD-00230 P3.4 moves host types into
TypeScript global modules. DataView could not move until typed arrays had
`buffer` and `byteOffset` (ADR-01351).

## Investigation

- Every DataView access is an aliasing byte copy plus an integer or float
  reinterpretation. A `Uint8Array` over the view's range, and one 8-byte
  scratch buffer viewed as `Float32Array`, `Float64Array`, `BigInt64Array`
  and `BigUint64Array`, express all of them.
- Node 24's DataView has `getFloat16`/`setFloat16`. Binary16 is converted
  in TypeScript with round-half-even.
- V8's messages: `Offset is outside the bounds of the DataView`, `Start
  offset N is outside the bounds of the buffer` and `Invalid DataView length
  N`. Its inspect output lists `[byteLength]`, `[byteOffset]` and `[buffer]`
  in brackets, on one line when they fit in 80 columns.
- The old codegen rejected a DataView as a Blob part and Buffer source,
  where Node accepts any BufferSource.

## Decision

1. **`lib/node/kml_dataview.ts`** is a global module exporting
   `class DataView`. It holds the constructor checks, every get/set width
   with the per-call endianness, Float16, `[inspect.custom]` and
   `Symbol.toStringTag`. TypeScript's own declarations still type it.
2. **Deleted:**
   - `NewDataViewExpression` and its sema, checker, walker and codegen
     entries;
   - the `IsDataView` flag, `DataViewType` and `emit_dataview.go`;
   - the 22 `@intrinsic DataView.prototype.*` annotations, the inspect
     hook and the dynamic-construct arity row.
   `llvm.bswap` and the conditional swap move to `emit_buffer.go`, whose
   `read*BE` accessors use them.
3. **Interop:**
   - `ArrayBuffer.isView` answers the class statically
     (`isGlobalClassInstance`) and through `any` (`emitAnyInstanceOfClass`).
   - A DataView Blob part contributes its range's bytes
     (`emitDataViewBytes`).

## Implementation notes

- **Library:** `lib/node/kml_dataview.ts`, and the annotations removed from
  `lib/es.d.ts`.
- **Front end:** `ast/nodes.go` (walker regenerated), `sema/builtin_new.go`
  and `checker/checker.go`.
- **Codegen:** `emit_blob.go`, `emit_buffer.go`, `emit_call.go`,
  `emit_classref.go`, `emit_dynamic.go`, `emit_exprs*.go`,
  `emit_func.go`, `emit_inspect*.go`, `emit_intrinsics.go`,
  `emit_lowered.go` and `types.go`.

## Side effects discovered

- Two shared-runtime bugs surfaced in the comparison with Node, each fixed
  in its own ADR:
  - a power of two printed 17 digits (ADR-01353);
  - a nested custom-inspect string was not re-indented (ADR-01354).
- `TextDecoder`'s `inputBytes` copies a typed array other than a
  `Uint8Array` through `__kml_native.typedBytes`. It could now view the
  bytes in place through `buffer`/`byteOffset`. Left as is, since it is
  correct.

## Verification

Mac (Apple Silicon), against Node 24, matching byte for byte:
- every get/set width and endianness, Float16 at the boundaries (65504,
  65520, the subnormals, -0, NaN, -Infinity);
- both RangeErrors, inspect at top level and nested;
- `isView` statically and through `any`, `instanceof`, `toString`;
- TextDecoder input and a Blob part.

Tests: new `TestE2EDataViewGlobalClass`, plus the DataView, ArrayBuffer,
TypedArray, Blob, inspect and encoding groups.
