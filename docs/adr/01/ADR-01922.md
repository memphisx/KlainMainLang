# ADR-01922: `new` through a namespace

- **Relations**: `Implements TDD-00230` (checker coverage)
- **Date**: 2026-10-21

## Context

`new Geometry.Point(1, 2)` with `Geometry` a namespace was left unanswered by the checker: the binder routed a qualified `new` through its member expression only when the qualifier was a variable, and otherwise looked the class up by its bare name, which is not in scope outside the namespace.

## Decision

A qualifier naming a namespace is routed through the member expression too, so the checker types the construction from the namespace's exported class.

## Consequences

- A namespace-qualified construction is typed as in tsc.
