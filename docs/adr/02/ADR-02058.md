# ADR-02058: Word characters under `iu`; the guard's wrapper is no user group

- **Date**: 2026-11-22

## Context

- Under the `i` and `u` (or `v`) flags, ES's word characters are those whose
  simple case folding is in `[0-9A-Z_a-z]`: ASCII plus U+017F and U+212A.
  PCRE2's `\w` is ASCII, so `/\w/iu.test("ſ")` was false (true in V8), and
  `\W`, `\b` and `\B` diverged the same way.
- `/((?:x|[a-z]))+\1/.exec("abss")` left group 1 undefined (V8: `"s"`).
  `empty_iter_guard` wraps a quantified capture that may match empty in
  `(?:(X)(?Cn))`; `iter_mark` then took that wrapper for a user's repeated
  group and unset captures from "earlier iterations". Its marks come from
  callouts that backtracking does not undo, so an iteration tried and
  abandoned counted as the last one.

## Decision

- `iu_words` (regexp.c) runs for a pattern with `i` and `u` or `v`. Outside
  a class, `\w` becomes `(?-i:[\w ſ K])`, `\W` becomes `(?-i:[^\w ſ K])` and
  `\b`/`\B` become lookarounds over that set. Inside a class, `\w` gains the
  two characters, and a class holding `\W` becomes an alternation with the
  complement (a negated one, a lookahead before the set). Caseless matching
  is off around the sets, so PCRE2's case closure adds no partner of a
  member (U+0131's `I`). The two characters are written as UTF-8:
  `ALT_BSUX` reads no `\x{…}`.
- `iter_mark` skips a group whose body ends with the guard's numeric
  callout `(?Cn)`. ES patterns have no callouts, so only the guard writes
  them.

## Consequences

- `TestE2ERegExpCaseInsensitiveUnicodeWord` matches Node, in and out of
  classes, under `v`, with replace and match.
- Left: a backreference past a repeated group still sees an earlier
  iteration's capture (`/(?:(a)|b)+\1/`). That needs a backtrack-safe
  record of each iteration's start.
- Verified on Node304 (Linux x86-64).
