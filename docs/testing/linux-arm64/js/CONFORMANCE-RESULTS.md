Generated by `tools/conformance` (TDD-00008 Design V2) against the full, unfiltered `tc39/test262` corpus — not a hand-picked subset. Regenerate with `make conformance` (see the README's "Test262 conformance" section). Do not hand-edit; re-run instead.
