# ADR-01071: `setTimeout`/`setInterval` clamp the delay to `[1, 2^31-1]` ms

- **Relations**: Extends ADR-01048 (which recorded the divergence)
- **Date**: 2026-09-22

## Context

BACKLOG §0 item 9: `setTimeout(f, 0)` fired with no delay; Node's timers (`lib/internal/timers.js`) coerce a
delay outside `[1, TIMEOUT_MAX]` — including an omitted, zero, negative or NaN delay — to 1 ms. 20,000
sequential zero-delay timers took 0.3 s here and ≥ 20 s in Node; only timing-observable, but it is the cadence
every polling loop written against Node assumes.

## Investigation

`timerDelayArg` (`emit_timers.go`) coerced the argument to i64 and passed it straight to
`@__kml_timer_schedule`; an omitted argument was `0`. `setImmediate` shares the queue with delay 0 and must
keep it (it is a different phase in Node, not a timer).

## Decision

`timerDelayArg` emits the clamp: `select (d < 1 || d > 2147483647) ? 1 : d`; an omitted delay is `1`. Only
`setTimeout`/`setInterval` go through it (the interval reuses the clamped value as its cadence, as Node's
`setInterval` does); `setImmediate` still schedules at 0.

## Implementation notes

`codegen/llvm/emit_timers.go`. A NaN double coerces through `fptosi` to an out-of-range integer and lands in the
clamp either way.

## Side effects discovered

None.

## Verification

Windows: 300 chained `setTimeout(step, 0)` take ≥ 250 ms (Node: the same), a 0-ms `setInterval` fires five
times and clears. E2E: `TestE2ETimerZeroDelayClamp` (`tests/timers_clamp_test.go`).
