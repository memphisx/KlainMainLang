# ADR-01882: a boxed-initializer binding read where the checker narrows it

- **Relations**: `Implements TDD-00230` (P3.3: the shadow report's Identifier bucket)
- **Date**: 2026-10-21

## Context

- An unannotated binding whose initializer codegen holds boxed (a field read a write through `any` may shadow, `const enc = this.encoding`) is a box. Codegen reads it unboxed where the checker narrows it to a primitive or a plain class instance (checkerNarrowed); the representation oracle answered the box at every read, at three separate rules, one of which re-boxed what another had unboxed.

## Decision

- The oracle's three rules for such a binding share one read (boxedInitRead) that applies codegen's checkerNarrowed through a hook.

## Consequences

- The stream Decoder row every program loading `stream` carried leaves the shadow report.
- Verified on Apple Silicon.
