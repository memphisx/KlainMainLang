# ADR-01767: Generator methods on the prototype object, and detached methods without a receiver

- **Relations**: `Implements TDD-00230`
- **Date**: 2026-10-20

## Context

Test262's arguments-object and class-method tests take a method off the prototype and call it bare (`var ref = C.prototype.method; ref(42, 'TC39')`). Two divergences:

- `C.prototype.g` was `undefined` for a generator or async generator method. Such a method holds no vtable slot, so it is left out of `MethodOrder`, which `emitClassProto` walked.
- A detached method called with a receiver that isn't a C instance threw "called on incompatible receiver" unless its body could be recompiled on an `any` receiver. Generator and async bodies never can. Node only fails when the body actually uses `this`.

## Decision

- `emitClassProto` adds the class's own generator methods after the slotted ones, in declaration order.
- `emitShapeMethodAdapter` skips the receiver check for a method whose body uses no `this`, `super` or private name (`readsReceiver`, conservative across nested functions). The body then runs with a null self, which it never reads.

## Consequences

- `TestE2EJSFileDetachedPrototypeMethods` covers both.
- Generator methods come after the other methods in `Object.getOwnPropertyNames(C.prototype)` when declared between them, not strictly in source order.
