# ADR-00613: Close the iterator on `break` out of a `for…of` over a generator

- **Relations**: Builds on [TDD-00086](../tdd/TDD-00086.md)/[ADR-00259](ADR-00259.md) (`.return()` runs enclosing finallys); completes [TDD-00061](../tdd/TDD-00061.md)
- **Date**: 2026-09-01

## Context

`break`ing out of a `for…of` loop over a generator left the generator's fiber
suspended and simply abandoned — the loop never invoked the iterator's
`.return()`, so an enclosing `finally` in the generator body (the idiomatic
resource-cleanup shape, especially over an infinite generator) never ran. JS's
`for…of` runs `IteratorClose` on any abrupt or early completion, which calls the
iterator's `return` method.

## Decision

Drive the generator's `.return()` at the loop-exit label of `emitForOfGenerator`.
`endL` is the single label reached by both a `break` and normal (`done`)
completion, so calling `emitGeneratorReturnByValue(gen, zero)` there covers both:

- **`break`** (generator still `started && !done`): `.return()` resumes the fiber
  in return-mode, whose yield-point handler runs the enclosing `finally` blocks
  and completes the generator — reusing the exact machinery an explicit
  `gen.return()` already uses.
- **Normal completion** (already `done`): `emitGeneratorReturnByValue` is a
  no-op on a done/not-started generator — it neither resumes the body nor re-runs
  a finally — so the finally runs exactly once, and emitting the call
  unconditionally at the shared exit label is safe.

Async generators are consumed by the separate `for await…of` path and are
untouched here.

## Scope

Covers `break` (and fall-through) out of a `for…of` over a sync generator. A
`return`/`throw` from *inside* the loop body exits through the function's own
return/exception path rather than `endL`, so it does not yet close the iterator —
a narrower, documented residual (JS closes it there too).

## Verification

`TestE2EForOfGeneratorBreakRunsFinally` (break runs the finally),
`TestE2EForOfInfiniteGeneratorBreakRunsFinally` (the classic infinite-generator
cleanup), and `TestE2EForOfGeneratorNormalCompletionFinallyOnce` (no double-run) —
all matching Node v26. A generator with no `finally` breaks cleanly, and the
generator / for-of suites still pass. Built and verified on Mac (Apple Silicon).
